How it behaves
1 · GatherOn load: dots fly in and land in 1.6 s. At big sizes a few papers turn into light first, like the sign-in.
2 · BreatheWhile visible: dots twinkle, the sky seed glows in and out, a soft light wave crosses the leaf every 6.5 s.
3 · Touch (C · papers)Hover: dots near the pointer turn back into the college papers they came from. Click / tap: every dot becomes a paper for a moment, then light again.
4 · Size rulesTouch effects only at 64 px and up (website, about, splash). Smaller: gather + twinkle only, solid leaf under the dots.
5 · Where it stays stillSidebar, app header, chat (AI states have their own motion), favicon, e-mails, print. Reduce motion: still dots everywhere.
6 · One engineSame canvas code as the sign-in panel and splash. Stops when the tab is hidden.
