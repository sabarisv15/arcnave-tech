Rules
One real inputOne text field under the 6 boxes: inputmode numeric, autocomplete “one-time-code”, label “6-digit code”. Paste and autofill work; screen readers hear one field.
Said before it happensThe line under the title says we check at digit 6, so the auto-check is not a surprise (WCAG 3.2.2).
Errors are read outMessage line is role alert. Colour is never alone: icon + words.
Never says who has an accountSame screen, same words, same timing for unknown e-mails. Pause and limit messages are the same for everyone.
Numbers from the build6 digits · 10 min · 5 wrong tries · 1 code / 60 s · 5 codes / hour · 15 min pause after 10 wrong / hour (N-72).
Grouped 3 + 3482 913 is easier to read and copy than 482913, in the mail and in the boxes.
