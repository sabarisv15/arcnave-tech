Evidence checked against official documentation and author papers available on **2 October 2026**.

- **Confirmed:** the source directly documents the component.
- **Partial:** it documents only part of the requested capability, or live verification was incomplete.
- **Not found:** **NOT FOUND IN OFFICIAL DOCS** reviewed here; this does not prove the capability cannot exist.
- **When needed:** applicability inferred from the documented purpose. “Always” means within the stated path, such as *indexed dense retrieval*.
- Vendor features are scoped to their documented products. Their inclusion does **not** establish availability through your particular Vertex AI endpoints.
