Founder-confirmed facts: founded 15 September 2025; no Google Cloud promotional credits

**What does your startup do?** ArcNave is an independent AI software startup building an
academic workspace for college teams. It brings conversations, documents, projects, and
editable outputs together so staff can organise evidence, identify gaps, and prepare
working drafts with human review.

**What problem are you solving?** Academic documentation and accreditation preparation
often require college teams to gather records from separate sources, compare them with
requirements, and manually prepare drafts and follow-up questions. ArcNave aims to make
that work more traceable and reviewable. Staff remain responsible for checking sources.

**Who are your customers?** Colleges and other higher-education institutions are the
intended subscription customers. IQAC teams, faculty, department heads, and administrators
are the initial users.

**What is your business model?** A planned recurring college/institution subscription
for access to the workspace and academic workflows. Pricing has not been set. No current
revenue is asserted.

**What makes the product different?** ArcNave combines general AI assistance with project
context, uploaded source documents, and editable outputs focused initially on NAAC, NBA,
and AICTE preparation. It is designed to expose missing evidence and questions for staff
review, rather than present generated material as an approved submission. The longer-term
direction connects AI actions with structured academic workflows.

**What stage is the product at?** In development. The public site, login, and signup entry
points are live. The repository contains accounts, chats, projects, files, and AI provider
integrations. Add a current production walkthrough before asserting that all of these
work together live. The illustrative website preview is not MVP proof.

**How will you use Google Cloud?** ArcNave's backend integrates with Vertex AI for model
requests and is built for Cloud Run services and isolated file-processing execution.
Cloud Storage is supported for applicable file and temporary staging paths, and
PostgreSQL stores workspace data. We expect usage to grow as we validate document and
academic workflows with colleges. Confirm actual production services before sending; do
not claim Cloud SQL or Secret Manager deployment without evidence.

**What is your product roadmap?** The current development stage focuses on a reliable
workspace and document workflows. The next stage improves source-linked drafts, file
handling, and review for NAAC, NBA, and AICTE preparation while gathering feedback from
college users. The longer-term direction extends the assistant into classes, students,
attendance, assessments, reporting, and other faculty workflows. These areas are planned,
not currently available features. Add committed milestones before submission.

**What is your plan for growth?** Start with document-heavy college teams, validate the
workflow and subscription offering with institutions, then extend to more academic
operations and colleges through a repeatable software product. Add actual acquisition
